## Brand & Style

This design system expresses authoritative reliability, operational precision, and uncompromising physical cargo security. The brand interfaces with enterprise procurement officers, supply chain managers, port operators, and freight directors who require rapid access to mission-critical specifications, load-securing calculators, and transit status.

The visual direction combines **Swiss-inspired Modernism** with an **Industrial Precision Aesthetic**:
- **Structured Precision**: Asymmetrical grid systems, fine technical hairline rules (0.5pt to 1pt), and data-forward modular cards reminiscent of container manifests and shipping manifests.
- **High-Trust Legibility**: Unapologetic clarity over decoration. Generous negative space within containers, paired with rigid structural borders and technical labels.
- **Engineered Utilitarianism**: Functional industrial safety accents (high-visibility safety orange) placed against deep maritime abyssal blues to communicate rigorous safety compliance and structural permanence.

## Colors

The palette is engineered to invoke deep maritime shipping environments punctuated by high-visibility safety hardware.

- **Primary (`#0B192C` - Deep Abyssal Navy)**: Represents structural integrity, marine freight, and institutional trust. Used for dominant typography, structural headers, high-contrast navigation shells, and primary interactive elements.
- **Secondary (`#1E3E62` - Technical Slate Blue)**: Represents logistical management and maritime operations. Applied to active states, secondary buttons, structural subheaders, borders in dark contexts, and categorized card headers.
- **Tertiary (`#E65100` - Industrial Safety Amber/Orange)**: Derived from industrial strapping, ISO cargo markers, and port safety equipment. Strictly reserved for high-priority calls to action, urgent operational alerts, primary telemetry warnings, and cargo security status indicators.
- **Neutral (`#F8FAFC` - Pristine Technical Canvas)**: A cold, ultra-clean white-gray ground that prevents eye strain across dense tabular datasets, spec sheets, and tracking consoles.
- **Surface Accents**:
  - Border hairline: `#E2E8F0` (light context), `#1E3E62` at 40% opacity (dark context).
  - Data telemetry: Emerald `#059669` for safe verification/intact seals; Crimson `#DC2626` for breached integrity/customs holds.

## Typography

The typographic system creates an intentional dialogue between geometric engineering and utilitarian readability:

1. **Headlines (`Space Grotesk`)**: Delivers technical authority, sharp terminals, and structural presence. Used for major section headers, product line declarations, and key impact statistics.
2. **Body Text (`Work Sans`)**: An exceptionally balanced neo-grotesque font rooted in Swiss functionalism. Delivers high optical clarity across dense paragraphs, technical specifications, and shipping documentation.
3. **Labels & Metadata (`JetBrains Mono`)**: Introduces precise industrial instrumentation styling. Applied to container tracking IDs, weight ratings (kg/daN), tensile tolerances, timestamp telemetries, and operational status chips.

All monospace micro-labels are transformed into uppercase with slight letter-spacing expansions to mimic stamped freight crates and laser-etched security seals.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model employs a responsive, fluid 12-column grid anchored by a structured 8pt baseline rhythm:

- **Desktop (>= 1200px)**: 12-column layout, `margin-lg` (3rem / 48px), `gutter-lg` (2rem / 32px). Maximum content containment at 1360px to preserve readability in wide-aspect industrial monitoring dashboards.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px)**: 8-column layout, `margin` (1.5rem / 24px), `gutter` (1.5rem / 24px). Dual-card side-by-side configurations collapse into stacked pairs.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column layout, `margin-sm` (1rem / 16px), `gutter-sm` (1rem / 16px). Data tables reflow into vertical spec cards.

### Spacing Rules
- Micro-spacing (`space-xs`, `space-sm`) is strictly applied to component-internal relationships (e.g., metric label to value, icon-to-text spacing).
- Medium spacing (`space-md`, `space-lg`) manages content padding within modular cards, container wrappers, and form groups.
- Macro-spacing (`space-xl`) isolates functional functional modules (e.g., separating the Cargo Load Calculator from the Physical Dimension Spec Matrix).

## Elevation & Depth

To honor industrial precision and avoid muddy, over-rendered interfaces, elevation relies primarily on **low-contrast technical outlines** and **crisp, architectural ambient shadows**:

- **Ground Level (Layer 0)**: `#F8FAFC` base page canvas.
- **Card Surface (Layer 1)**: Pure white `#FFFFFF` bounded by a 1px solid hairline border of `#E2E8F0`. Shadow is microscopic: `0 1px 3px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.04), 0 1px 2px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.02)`.
- **Raised Interactive Layer (Layer 2)**: For dropdown menus, hovered cards, and active tools. Bounded by a 1px border of `#CBD5E1` with an ambient shadow: `0 8px 16px -4px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -2px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.03)`.
- **Overlaid Dialogs & Drawers (Layer 3)**: Modals, consignment inspect drawers, and emergency alerts. 1px border tinted with secondary blue `#1E3E62` (20% opacity) and backed by `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.15), 0 10px 10px -5px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.04)`.
- **Technical Grid Overlays**: Critical summary cards utilize subtle 1px dashed grid accents (`#E2E8F0`) at corners or headers to evoke certified engineering blueprints.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a **Controlled Modern Roundedness (`2`)** profile to balance industrial durability with humanized software design:

- **Base Radius (`0.5rem` / 8px)**: Applied to input inputs, operational buttons, status badges, and sub-components. Prevents the severe, clinical harshness of completely sharp corners while preserving structural discipline.
- **Container Radius (`rounded-lg` / `1rem` / 16px)**: Applied to product specification cards, analytics dashboards, and interactive calculators.
- **Hero & Dialog Radius (`rounded-xl` / `1.5rem` / 24px)**: Applied to high-level system modals, floating drawer panels, and primary hero banners.
- **Zero Radius Exception**: Data tables, raw spec matrices, and technical status bars retain completely squared internal borders (`0px`) to maintain pure vertical and horizontal datum lines.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Tertiary Safety Orange)**: Fill `#E65100`, text `#FFFFFF`, border none, radius `0.5rem`. Hover: `#C2410C`. Focus: 3px outer ring `#FED7AA`. Used exclusively for critical calls to action (e.g., "Request Cargo Assessment", "Lock Manifest").
- **Secondary Action (Primary Navy)**: Fill `#0B192C`, text `#FFFFFF`, radius `0.5rem`. Hover: `#1E3E62`. Focus: 2px ring `#94A3B8`.
- **Technical Outline**: Background transparent, border 1px solid `#CBD5E1`, text `#0B192C`. Hover: Background `#F1F5F9`, border `#1E3E62`.

### Cards & Industrial Panels
- Background `#FFFFFF`, border 1px solid `#E2E8F0`, corner radius `1rem`.
- **Header Variant**: Features a top technical identification bar set in `JetBrains Mono` (`label-md`) containing SKU, serial sequence, or payload category, separated by a 1px divider rule `#F1F5F9`.

### Chips & Badges
- Fixed height 24px or 28px, corner radius `0.5rem`, typography `label-md` uppercase.
- **Intact / Approved**: Background `#ECFDF5`, text `#065F46`, border 1px solid `#A7F3D0`.
- **Dynamic Load Alert**: Background `#FFF7ED`, text `#9A3412`, border 1px solid `#FFEDD5`.
- **Critical Failure**: Background `#FEF2F2`, text `#991B1B`, border 1px solid `#FECACA`.

### Form Fields & Inputs
- Background `#FFFFFF`, border 1px solid `#CBD5E1`, radius `0.5rem`, padding `0.75rem 1rem`.
- Text styled with `body-md` in `#0B192C`. Placeholder styled in `#94A3B8`.
- Focus state: border-color `#1E3E62`, box-shadow `0 0 0 3px rgba(30, 62, 98, 0.12)`.
- Numeric inputs (e.g., weight, strapping tension, pallet units) feature inline unit tags styled in `JetBrains Mono`.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Size 18x18px, border 1.5px solid `#94A3B8`, radius `0.25rem` (checkbox) or circular (radio).
- Checked state: Fill `#0B192C`, icon white.

### Specialized Logistics Components
- **Payload Meter**: Horizontal segmented bar displaying linear force tolerances, color-coded across safe (Deep Blue `#1E3E62`), warning (Safety Orange `#E65100`), and critical thresholds.
- **Spec Data Table**: Compact 40px row height, subtle horizontal zebra-striping with `#F8FAFC`, right-aligned monospaced numerical figures, and fixed headers with 1px bottom border in `#0B192C`.
